Note on Sources and Methodology

This work uses a three-tier source classification to identify source type and evidentiary role: Tier 1 consists of official and primary-source records; Tier 2 consists of scholarly and empirical sources; Tier 3 consists of journalism, advocacy research, documentary materials, and commentary. The tier label does not by itself establish the truth, representativeness, causation, or sufficiency of a claim; claims are evaluated individually against the evidence supporting them. Complete methodology, limitations, and source-selection standards appear in Appendix C.
