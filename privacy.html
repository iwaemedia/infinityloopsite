Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information The Infinity Loop interactive reader (the "Service") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the Service, you agree to the practices described here.

1.Information We Collect

  • Account information — the name and email address you provide when starting a trial or signing in.
  • License & device data — a device identifier and license status used to grant and verify access on your device.
  • Usage information — basic technical data such as reading progress stored on your device and general activity needed to operate the Service.
  • Payment information — when you make a purchase, payment is handled by a third-party processor; we do not store your full payment-card details.

2.How We Use Information

  • To create and manage your account, trial, and license.
  • To deliver content and verify that your access is valid on your device.
  • To respond to support requests and communicate important updates.
  • To send occasional product updates where you have agreed to receive them.
  • To protect the Service against fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access.

3.Local Storage on Your Device

The Service stores certain information — such as your access status, reading progress, and a device identifier — locally in your browser so the reader works across sessions. Clearing your browser storage may sign you out or reset your local reading progress.

4.Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with service providers who help us operate the Service — such as our hosting provider and payment processor — and only to the extent needed to provide their services. We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect our rights and the safety of our users.

5.Email Communications

If you opt in to receive updates, you can unsubscribe at any time using the link in our emails or by contacting us. Transactional messages related to your account, trial, or license may still be sent as needed to operate the Service.

6.Data Retention

We retain account and license information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. You may request deletion of your account information as described below.

7.Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete the personal information we hold about you, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below. We will respond in accordance with applicable law.

8.Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

9.Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Significant changes may be communicated through the Service or by email where appropriate.

10.Contact
